Reynt maintenance texts

Operated by Nik Turpin, independent property manager

Reynt is the text messaging service Nik Turpin uses with his tenants and maintenance vendors. Tenants text to report maintenance problems and get updates on their requests. Vendors get job requests and reply to accept them, give arrival times and mark them done.

How to opt in

Give your mobile number to your property manager on your rental application, lease, tenant contact form or vendor agreement. You can also text the Reynt number to report a maintenance problem. Giving your number means you agree to this statement:

By providing your mobile number, you agree to receive maintenance and service text messages from Reynt about your rental. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.

Consent to receive texts is not a condition of renting or of any purchase. Your number is never sold or shared for marketing.
