Reynt maintenance texts

Operated by Nik Turpin, independent property manager

Reynt is the text messaging service Nik Turpin uses with tenants and maintenance vendors. Tenants text to report maintenance problems and get updates on their requests. Vendors get job requests and reply to accept them, give arrival times and mark them done.

How to opt in

Reynt texts are sent only to people who text the Reynt number first.

1. Tenants: text us first. Tenants can report a maintenance problem by texting:

(737) 258-3742

By texting this number, you agree to receive maintenance text messages from Reynt about your request. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out. Consent is not a condition of renting or of any purchase.

2. Vendors: text JOIN. Maintenance vendors who work with the property manager text JOIN to (737) 258-3742 before they receive any job requests. By texting JOIN, you agree to receive job request and job update text messages from Reynt. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.

Your first reply from Reynt confirms that you're enrolled and repeats the HELP and STOP instructions.

Your number is never sold or shared for marketing. See the SMS Terms and Privacy Policy.
