Privacy Policy

Reynt, operated by Nik Turpin · Effective October 24, 2026

This policy explains how Reynt (“we”, “us”) handles information when tenants, vendors and the property manager use our maintenance text messaging and email service.

Information we collect

  • Name, mobile phone number, email address, and property and unit details you or your property manager provide.
  • The content of texts and emails you send to Reynt, such as maintenance descriptions, and the messages we send you.
  • Delivery records from our messaging providers, such as whether a message was delivered.

How we use it

We use this information only to run maintenance requests: to receive and understand reports, send status updates, send job requests to vendors, get the property manager's approval, and keep a record of each request. An automated assistant may help sort and summarize maintenance reports.

How we share it

We share information only as needed to handle a maintenance request. For example, a vendor assigned to a job receives the property address, the unit, a description of the issue and the tenant's name and phone number. We use service providers to deliver messages (Twilio for SMS, Resend for email) and to help process maintenance reports. They may use the information only to provide those services to us.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Retention and security

We keep maintenance records for as long as needed to manage the rental property and meet legal obligations. We protect them with reasonable safeguards, including access controls and private network access.

Your choices

Reply STOP to any Reynt text to stop receiving texts, or HELP for help. To ask about, correct or delete your information, email reynt.help@gmail.com.

Changes

If we update this policy, we will post the new version here with a new effective date.
