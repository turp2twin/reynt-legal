SMS Terms and Conditions

Reynt, operated by Nik Turpin · Effective October 24, 2026

Program

Reynt Maintenance Texts. Transactional maintenance texts between a property manager, their tenants and their maintenance vendors. Tenants report maintenance problems and get status updates. Vendors get job requests and reply to accept, decline, give arrival times or mark jobs done. The property manager gets approval requests. Reynt does not send marketing messages.

Opting in

You opt in by giving your mobile number to the property manager and agreeing to receive maintenance texts, or by texting the Reynt number to report a problem. Consent is not a condition of renting or of any purchase.

Message frequency and cost

Message frequency varies with maintenance activity. Message and data rates may apply.

Opting out and help

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving texts. You will get one confirmation message, and then no more texts unless you reply START to opt back in. Reply HELP for help, or email turp2twin@gmail.com.

Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Emergencies

Reynt texts are not monitored around the clock. For fire, gas leaks or other emergencies, call 911 first.

Privacy

See our Privacy Policy. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
